AI Pricing Transparency

The EnergyShare AI determines electricity prices based on real-time neighborhood supply and demand conditions.

Pricing is influenced by:

• Renewable energy availability in the local network
• Household demand peaks
• Weather forecasts and expected solar generation
• Grid congestion and import dependency
• Historical consumption patterns

The AI prioritizes locally generated renewable energy first, helping households reduce carbon emissions while minimizing overall energy costs.

During periods of high renewable production, prices automatically decrease to encourage sustainable energy consumption.
